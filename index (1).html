A different kind of evening

Most parties ask you to shout over the music just to learn someone's name. This one doesn't.

Game Night is a small, carefully chosen gathering for people who like being around people. The room stays calm. The music stays low. Nobody is drinking, nobody is performing, and nobody has to pretend to be someone they're not. What you get is a table, a good crowd, and a set of games that turn strangers into teammates faster than any icebreaker ever could.

You'll accuse your new friend of being the Mafia and then laugh about it. You'll act out a movie title in front of people you met an hour ago. You'll find out who is quietly brilliant, who is secretly hilarious, and who you want to keep in touch with long after the evening is over.

It's designed to be the kind of night where you leave with real conversations, new contacts you actually want to message, and the feeling that you spent your Saturday well. Come curious. Come as you are.

How the evening unfolds

About four hours, start to finish, with a natural rhythm so you're never stuck wondering what to do next.

  1. Arrive and settle in

    Check in, collect your refreshment, and find your spot. The room is warm, relaxed and easy to walk into, even if you're coming alone.

  2. Break the ice

    A short welcome from your host, then light, fast games that get everyone talking and laughing before the main event begins.

  3. The main games

    Group games with real stakes and real laughs: bluffing, guessing, acting, and a little friendly betrayal. Everyone plays, and no experience is needed.

  4. Stay and connect

    As the games wind down, the conversations keep going. This is the part where new contacts turn into real connections.

What makes it work

  • Alcohol-free. Everyone is clear-headed, present and able to remember the night.
  • Low music. Soft background sound only, so you can hear every joke and every accusation.
  • Small and chosen. Thirty people who were thought of personally, not a crowd of whoever showed up.
  • Everyone plays. No spectators, no wallflowers. If you can laugh, you can play.
  • Come ready to meet people. Bring your energy, your curiosity and your phone for contacts.

Questions you might have

What does the pass cost?

The pass is ₦5,000 and includes a refreshment of your choice. Confirmed guests will pick their refreshment ahead of the day, so it's ready when you arrive.

I've never played Mafia. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Every game is explained before it begins, and you'll pick it up within one round. Beginners are often the most entertaining players.

Can I come alone?

Yes, and many people will. The games are built to mix the room, so you'll be part of a group within minutes.

Is it an all-night thing?

No. It starts at 4:00 PM and runs for about four hours, so you can enjoy your evening and still get home comfortably.
